Citizens Lens explanation Short answer: No. The police must tell you why you are being arrested. This is your right under the Constitution. There are limited exceptions, listed below.
Read more

Personal liberty means you cannot be locked up without a lawful reason. If arrested, you must be told the reason promptly and brought before a court within a reasonable time. Some situations (like a court order) have extra rules.

Original constitutional provision Section 35, 1999 Constitution (as amended)
"Every person shall be entitled to his personal liberty and no person shall be deprived of such liberty save in the following cases..."
Source / version Chapter IV • Section 35 • Status: current law • Reviewed 2026-09-27
Note: This is constitutional information, not legal advice. Other laws and court decisions can also apply.
